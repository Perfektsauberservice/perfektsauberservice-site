# Max Emotion Library

This file defines Max's emotional acting states.

## Core emotions

### Happy
- eyes bright
- smile open
- brows lifted softly
- body light and open

### Proud
- chest lifted
- chin slightly up
- confident smile
- steady stance

### Excited
- faster energy
- brighter eyes
- stronger brows
- bigger grin
- quicker gesture timing

### Curious
- attentive eyes
- slight head tilt
- one brow slightly lifted
- smaller mouth movement

### Reassuring
- soft smile
- calm eyes
- low gesture intensity
- stable shoulders

### Focused
- target-based eye line
- less smile
- compact concentration
- stronger action intent

### Surprised
- widened eyes
- quick brow lift
- brief open mouth
- body reacts before settling

### Playful
- lively eyes
- light grin
- small expressive head movement
- relaxed attitude

### Helpful
- open face
- calm clarity
- inviting gesture
- friendly confidence

## Emotion usage rules

- do not switch emotion randomly
- emotion should follow the situation
- face, body, and speech must match
- strong emotion needs a visible setup and release
- subtle emotion is often better for brand scenes
