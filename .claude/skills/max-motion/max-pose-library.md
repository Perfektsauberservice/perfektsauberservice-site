# Max Pose Library

This file defines Max's default body language and reusable pose base.

## Global pose rules

All Max poses should feel:
- balanced
- readable
- energetic
- slightly asymmetrical
- mascot-friendly
- clean in silhouette

Avoid:
- mannequin symmetry
- locked joints
- flat shoulders
- floating feet
- random limb placement

## Core reusable poses

### Pose 01 — Neutral idle
- feet shoulder-width apart
- weight slightly on one leg
- relaxed shoulders
- small smile
- attentive eyes

### Pose 02 — Friendly welcome
- chest open
- one hand lifted
- body slightly angled toward viewer
- open smile

### Pose 03 — Hero stance
- feet planted wider
- one hand on hip
- chest open
- confident smile

### Pose 04 — Viewer point
- arm extended toward viewer
- clear finger point
- supportive torso twist
- strong eye contact

### Pose 05 — Run contact
- front leg reaching
- back leg pushing
- opposite arm forward
- torso leaning into motion

### Pose 06 — Run passing pose
- hips shifting naturally
- leg transition under body
- arms passing
- lifted body rhythm

### Pose 07 — Hard stop
- one foot planted
- rear leg catching balance
- torso reacting to momentum
- one arm counterbalancing

### Pose 08 — Sledgehammer ready
- grounded stance
- knees bent slightly
- both hands gripping tool
- torso twisted for stored energy

### Pose 09 — Sledgehammer strike
- full-body commitment
- readable diagonal action line
- eyes on target
- strong emphasis

### Pose 10 — Follow-through
- action continues past contact
- knees absorb force
- hips and shoulders offset

### Pose 11 — Triumphant finish
- grin wide
- chest lifted
- arms lifted or opened
- grounded legs

### Pose 12 — Explaining gesture
- one hand open
- supportive head tilt
- calm shoulders
- friendly smile

### Pose 13 — Reassuring helper
- smaller gestures
- soft smile
- stable stance
- warm eye contact

### Pose 14 — Listening pose
- mouth gently closed
- attentive eyes
- slight lean toward speaker
- quiet hands

### Pose 15 — Happy reaction
- bright eyes
- open smile
- small body lift
- shoulder energy

### Pose 16 — Tool lean pose
- resting against tool
- relaxed elbow support
- friendly attitude

## Transition rules

When moving between poses:
- one body part leads
- the spine participates
- arms and head offset slightly
- balance is preserved
- nothing snaps all at once
