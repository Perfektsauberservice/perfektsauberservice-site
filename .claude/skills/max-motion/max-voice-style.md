# Max Voice Style Guide

This file defines how Max speaks, sounds, reacts, and expresses himself.

## Core voice identity

Max is:
- warm
- bright
- friendly
- clear
- lightly playful
- reassuring
- natural
- easy to understand

He should never sound:
- robotic
- too formal
- too adult
- too childish
- too rough or macho
- too aggressive
- too salesy
- too theatrical
- like a handyman or rough worker
- like a 40-year-old technician
- like a pushy ad script

## Speaking rhythm

Max speaks in short, clear, warm phrases.

Preferred rhythm:
- one main idea per sentence
- small natural pauses
- emphasis on meaningful words
- soft and pleasant sentence endings
- easy and friendly listening flow

## Phrasing to avoid

Never use phrasing that sounds:
- rough or physical ("ich pack mit an", "wir schleppen", "wir reissen raus")
- over-macho or boastful ("ich hab schon schlimmere gesehen")
- cold call / salesman ("Ruf einfach an — wir kommen", "Tu nu miști nimic")
- generic ad copy ("schnell, sauber, unkompliziert" as a mechanical list)
- transactional or pushy

These patterns make Max sound like a worker or salesperson, not a mascot.

## Preferred phrasing approach

Max describes help as something pleasant and easy for the customer.
He focuses on the feeling of relief, not the physical labor.

Think:
- "Wir kümmern uns darum" — not "wir schleppen alles raus"
- "Du musst dir keine Sorgen machen" — not "du musst nichts tun"
- "Alles wird aufgeräumt" — not "wir räumen alles raus"

The customer should feel taken care of, not sold to.

## Delivery types

### Friendly welcome
- open smile
- light upward energy
- relaxed pace
- inviting eye contact
- warm and pleasant, not energetic-aggressive

### Explaining
- clear articulation
- gentle hand support
- calm and easy pacing
- one idea per sentence
- never a list of features

### Excited action
- brighter tone
- slightly faster pace
- playful, not rough
- visible grin, not warrior face

### Reassuring
- calmer pace
- softer smile
- stable eye contact
- low-pressure warmth
- never commanding

## Natural speech rules

To sound natural, Max should:
- vary sentence length slightly
- not stress every word
- pause briefly before important ideas
- sound like a friendly person talking, not an ad being read
- avoid slogans that sound pasted on
- avoid imperative commands toward the customer

## Good phrasing examples

### German
- "Hallo! Ich bin Max — ich helfe, wenn's zu Hause voll geworden ist."
- "Kein Problem, das schaffen wir zusammen."
- "Wir kümmern uns um alles — du musst dir keine Gedanken machen."
- "Ob Keller, Dachboden oder Wohnung — wir sind dabei."
- "Es wird alles ordentlich und sauber hinterlassen."

### Romanian
- "Salut! Sunt Max — sunt aici sa ajut cand e prea mult de facut singur."
- "Ne ocupam noi de tot, fara griji."
- "Lasam totul curat si in ordine."
- "Fie ca e vorba de pivnita, pod sau apartament — suntem acolo."
- "E simplu: ne dai un semn, si ne ocupam."

### English
- "Hi! I'm Max — I help when things pile up at home."
- "We take care of everything, so you don't have to worry."
- "Everything gets sorted out, clean and tidy."
- "Basement, attic, apartment — we're there."
- "Just let us know, and we'll handle it."

## Brand-safe speech

Max should sound:
- helpful
- trustworthy
- positive
- warm and approachable
- like a friendly local mascot

He should not sound:
- sarcastic
- arrogant
- rude
- creepy
- chaotic
- threatening
- like a sales machine

## Default voice formula

Use by default:
- tone: warm, bright, and friendly
- pace: medium
- smile level: medium-high
- clarity: high
- gesture energy: medium
- facial expressiveness: medium-high
- emotional register: pleasant and reassuring
