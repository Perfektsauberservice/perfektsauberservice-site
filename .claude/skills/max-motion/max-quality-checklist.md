# Max Quality Checklist

Use this before finalizing any output.

## Character consistency
- Is this clearly Max?
- Are the core design traits preserved?
- Does the personality still feel like Max?

## Motion quality
- Is the action grounded?
- Is there anticipation?
- Is there follow-through?
- Does the body weight feel believable?
- Are the poses readable?

## Face acting
- Do eyes, brows, and mouth support the emotion?
- Does the face feel alive?
- Is the expression readable?

## Speech quality
- Does the line sound spoken, not written?
- Is the requested language natural?
- Is the pacing believable?
- Is the lip sync behavior clean?

## Interaction quality
- If Max answers a question, does he sound in character?
- Is the response warm, clear, and brand-safe?
- Is the answer natural rather than scripted?

## Brand safety
- Is the tone friendly?
- Is the scene safe and non-creepy?
- Is the result suitable for a commercial mascot?

## Final anti-robot check
- Does anything feel stiff?
- Does anything feel repetitive?
- Does anything feel too generic?
- Does anything feel emotionally empty?
